Frequently asked questions

How do I add an Android Home Screen widget?

  1. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen.
  2. Tap Widgets.
  3. Choose John 1:1.
  4. Add Daily Verse, Daily Reading, or Reading Progress.

Tap the widget to open the passage or your reading progress. Android can delay widget updates to save battery.

How do I add an iPhone Lock Screen widget?

  1. Touch and hold your Lock Screen.
  2. Tap Customize.
  3. Tap the widget area under the clock.
  4. Choose John 1:1.
  5. Add Daily Verse, Daily Reading, Reading Progress, or Verse Countdown.
  6. Pick the rectangular size for full text, or the inline/circular size for a compact view.

The sizes available depend on the widget. You can also add widgets to your Home Screen.

How often does the verse change?

In Settings → Verse rotation, choose every 10, 15, or 30 minutes, every hour, every few hours, or once a day. Your device controls when a widget can update.

Can I show verses from only the Old or New Testament?

Yes. In Settings → Verse pool, pick Full Bible, Old Testament, or New Testament. The Old Testament option includes the deuterocanonical books.

Which Bible editions are included?

Choose the World English Bible, Catholic Edition, Douay–Rheims, or Latin Vulgate in Settings → Translation. All three editions are included in the app.

Does it work offline?

The Bible, prayers, and reading content work offline. No John account is required. Android read aloud requires an installed offline voice. External websites and Rosary videos require a network connection. Device dictation can also use a network service.

Where are my notes and reading data stored?

John saves them on your device. It has no ads or tracking software. Android excludes app data from automatic cloud backup and device transfer. You can move saved passages and notes with Export and Import. On iOS, device backup settings apply. If iCloud sync is available and you enable it, selected saved data is copied to your private iCloud database. Dictation, shared files, and external sites use their providers' privacy policies. See our Privacy Policy.

Why does Android read aloud ask me to install a voice?

John uses an installed offline English voice for English Scripture. It uses an installed offline Latin voice for the Vulgate. If your speech service has no suitable offline voice, install one in your device's text-to-speech settings. Voice availability depends on the provider.

Why did a reminder arrive late?

Your device schedules reminders locally. Android can delay them to save battery. Allow notifications for John 1:1 in device settings. In the app, check the reminder's delivery time and confirm that it is enabled.
