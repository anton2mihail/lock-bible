Privacy Policy

Lock Bible · Last updated June 26, 2026

The short version

Lock Bible does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app works entirely on your device and makes no network requests. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Information we collect

None. We do not ask for, gather, or receive any personal data — no name, email, contacts, location, device identifiers, or usage analytics.

Information stored on your device

Your in-app preferences (such as your chosen verse rotation interval and verse pool) are saved locally on your device so the app and its widgets work the way you set them. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or anyone else. Removing the app deletes it.

Network & offline use

The complete Bible text is bundled inside the app. Lock Bible functions fully offline and does not connect to any server during normal use.

Third parties

Lock Bible contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share data with anyone, because we do not collect any.

Children's privacy

Lock Bible is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will post the updated version on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
