Privacy Policy

John 1:1 · Last updated October 13, 2026

About this policy

This policy covers John 1:1 for Android and iOS, published by North Boutique Studios Ltd. The app stores your reading data on your device. It has no app account, ads, purchases, analytics, or tracking software. North Boutique does not operate a server that receives your notes or reading history.

Data saved on your device

The app saves your settings, reading position, reading progress, recent searches, bookmarks, highlights, tags, notes, note drawings, prayer favorites, novena progress and intentions, and daily reflections. It uses this data to provide the features you choose. Notes and reflections can include personal or religious information that you enter.

The app keeps saved data in storage managed by your device's operating system. Saved data remains there until you delete it or remove the app's data. Use your device's screen lock to help protect access.

Private notes in the Confession guide are temporary. The app does not save them in its library. The app clears them when you leave the guide or put the app in the background.

Device backup and transfer

On Android, John 1:1 excludes its saved data from automatic cloud backup and device transfer. To move your saved passages and notes, use Export and Import. If you select a cloud storage location for an export, that provider's privacy policy applies.

On iOS, device backups can include app data under your Apple backup settings. Where the app offers iCloud sync, it is off by default. If you turn it on, the app copies notes, bookmarks, highlights, tags, and reading progress to a private CloudKit database in your Apple account. Turning sync off does not delete an existing iCloud copy.

Voice features

On Android, the Dictate button opens the speech recognition service selected on your device. Before it opens, the app explains that the provider can use a network service. If you continue, that service records your voice and returns text to the app. It can send audio to its servers. Its own privacy policy and device settings apply. John 1:1 does not save an audio recording. You can type a note without using dictation.

Android read aloud passes Scripture text to an installed offline voice in your device's text-to-speech service. If no suitable offline voice is installed, the app asks you to install one. The speech provider's policy applies to its service. You can stop playback from the app or its media notification.

On iOS, the app's note dictation button requires microphone and speech permission. It uses recognition on the device and does not save the audio. Your keyboard's dictation feature is a separate system service with its own settings.

Sharing and exported files

If you choose Share, the app passes the selected text or Scripture image to the app you choose. If you choose Export, the app writes your saved passages, notes, and tags to the location you choose. That location can be a cloud storage service. Those apps and services use their own privacy policies. John 1:1 does not send a copy to North Boutique. Import reads only the backup file you choose.

Offline reading, links, and reminders

The Bible text, prayers, and reading content are included in the app. Reading this content does not require a network connection. Reminders are scheduled on your device. Android notification permission controls reminder delivery. The app uses a media service to keep read aloud active in the background.

Links to official readings, Rosary videos, and other sources open a browser or another app. Those services can receive your network address and information about the page or video you open. Their privacy policies apply. This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub's privacy policy applies when you visit the website.

Data removal

You can delete saved items and notes in the app. You can reset reading progress in its settings. On Android, Clear storage in device settings removes the app's local saved data. Removing the app removes its local data, but it does not remove files you exported, copies you shared, or existing cloud backups. Use the relevant backup or storage service to manage those copies. On iOS, a device backup can restore old app data when you install the app again. On either platform, importing a file can restore the data in that file.

Support

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you send. We use it to answer your request. Do not send private notes unless they are needed for the request. To ask about or request removal of information you sent to support, email contact@north.boutique.

Children

The app does not ask for a user's age or create a user profile. The same local storage and optional service rules apply to all users. Device backup, speech services, and external sites have their own terms and age rules.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will post the updated version on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
