# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who want Scripture to be present throughout the day without creating an account or relying on connectivity. They may glance at a lock screen, open a verse one-handed, or settle into longer reading in varied lighting and with different text-size needs.

## Product Purpose

Lock Bible keeps a complete Catholic Bible and a rotating verse experience on the device. Success means the widget reliably surfaces Scripture, every verse can be opened in context, and readers can comfortably continue where they left off without setup or network access.

## Brand Personality

Calm, reverent, and approachable. The interface should feel native and trustworthy, with the Scripture carrying more visual weight than the controls.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a social feed, a gamified habit tracker, an ornate devotional product, or a settings-heavy technical utility. Avoid accounts, streak pressure, decorative motion, and controls that interrupt reading.

## Design Principles

1. Keep every core reading workflow fully functional offline.
2. Make continuation and context effortless.
3. Reveal controls progressively so Scripture remains the focus.
4. Prefer familiar platform behavior over custom interaction patterns.
5. Store reading state privately on the device and communicate that clearly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Dynamic Type-friendly layouts, VoiceOver labels and selected states, comfortable touch targets, reduced-motion expectations, light and dark appearances, and readable typography for both brief glances and extended reading.
