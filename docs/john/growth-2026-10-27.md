What the Play numbers say: almost nobody sees the listing (5 visitors in 28 days). The first problem on Android is
being found, not converting. A store listing experiment cannot give a result at this traffic, so it waits until the
listing has visitors.

What the App Store numbers say: the app is seen (about 320 impressions a day) and search brings most of the
downloads that report a source. About 1 in 63 impressions becomes a download. Better product page assets and keywords
move this number; paid search can be measured against it.

**Paid promotion (27 Oct): Anton's decision is no paid ads for now.** Wait for the nominations and the organic
numbers (Advent, then January) first. If a test is wanted later, the measurable one is Apple Search Ads on
exact-match Catholic terms with a small fixed budget for Advent (29 Nov – 24 Dec), compared with this baseline. Google
Ads app campaigns wait until the Play listing converts the few visitors it has.

1.6 million monthly users or US$40,000 a month of spend, and Books & Reference is not a supported category.

Problems in the live default listing:

- It says "John 1:1 is free, with no ads or in-app purchases". Since 1.2.0 (in review) there is one in-app purchase,

Store copy rules kept: no competitor names, no "#1", the WEB Catholic Edition named as a study translation, the
prayer sources stated without claiming approval for the texts still under licence request.
