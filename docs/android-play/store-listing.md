## Full description

```text
John 1:1 is a Catholic Bible and prayer app for every day: Scripture, the Rosary, the readings of the day, the saints, and the writings of the Fathers and Doctors of the Church. It works offline, with no account and no ads.

ADVENT WITH THE FATHERS
From the first Sunday of Advent, Today shows the week of Advent and a Father's comment on the day's Gospel from the Catena Aurea of St Thomas Aquinas. The Advent page has the Advent wreath with the Collect of each Sunday from the Roman Missal, Rorate caeli, and the O Antiphons in Latin and English from 17 December.

THE FATHERS AND DOCTORS
An offline library of 275 works by 26 authors: St Augustine, St John Chrysostom, St Ambrose, St Athanasius, St Leo the Great, St Thomas Aquinas and more. The Catena Aurea on all four Gospels sets the Fathers beside every Gospel of the year. The first part of every work, the Apostolic Fathers and the Confessions of St Augustine are free to read. One optional purchase unlocks the whole library.

THE BIBLE
All 73 books in three complete editions: the Douay-Rheims, the Latin Vulgate, and the World English Bible, Catholic Edition (a study translation). Search every verse offline. Save bookmarks, highlights and notes, and share a passage as a Scripture card.

PRAYER
The Rosary step by step, with a painting for each mystery. Seven novenas, traditional prayers and prayers of the saints, each shown with its source. Lectio divina and a guide to prepare for Confession.

EVERY DAY
The calendar and reading references of the Roman Rite in the United States, the saints of the day, reading plans, reminders and home screen widgets.

John 1:1 is free, with no ads and no account. One optional in-app purchase unlocks the full Fathers library. The Bible, the Rosary, prayers, readings and saints stay free.

Support: contact@north.boutique
```

## Graphic assets

Run `python3 scripts/android/store-assets.py` from the repo root.
The script uses Pillow and the existing Lora and Space Grotesk fonts in
`node_modules/@expo-google-fonts`. The repo's normal dependency install supplies
these fonts. The script generates only the icon, feature graphic, listing JSON,
and their manifest. It does not make or change app screenshots.

| Asset | File | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Store icon | `assets/play-store/icon-512.png` | 512 × 512, RGBA PNG |
| Feature graphic | `assets/play-store/feature-1024x500.png` | 1024 × 500, RGB PNG |
| Listing fields | `assets/play-store/listing-en-US.json` | JSON with character counts |
| Source and output hashes | `assets/play-store/manifest.json` | SHA-256 hashes |

The icon is the John icon at the Play size: a detail of the 6th-century Christ
Pantocrator icon at Sinai, a public-domain photograph from Wikimedia Commons.
Google applies its corner mask and outside shadow. The feature graphic is a
plain crop of Abraham Bloemaert's *The Four Evangelists* (public domain). It has
no text, overlay, or drawn shape. It does not show a simulated app screen. No
store graphic is generated by AI. The sources are recorded in
`assets/CREDITS.md`. Answer any asset-origin question in Console with this
source information.

two screenshots. Check the visible screen content separately before upload.

## Verified Play requirements

The title limit is 30 characters. The short description limit is 80. The full
description limit is 4000. [Create and set up your app](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859152?hl=en)

The icon must be a 512 × 512, 32-bit PNG under 1024 KB. The feature graphic must
be a 1024 × 500 JPEG or RGB PNG. Phone screenshots must be JPEG or RGB PNG, with
each side from 320 to 3840 pixels. The longest side must be no more than twice
the shortest. Four 1080 × 1920 portrait captures help qualify an app for
recommendation formats. [Preview asset requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en)

Keep the icon square. Do not add rounded corners or an outside shadow.
[Google Play icon specifications](https://developer.android.com/distribute/google-play/resources/icon-design-specifications)
