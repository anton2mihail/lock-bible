# Message templates

Slots are `{{name}}` and `{{specific}}`. `{{specific}}` is one true, concrete
detail about that outlet, researched and carrying a source URL — not flattery.

A message with an empty `{{specific}}` does not get rendered. That is
deliberate: if we cannot say something specific, we have no business writing.

There was a `{{fit}}` slot for "why your audience would care". It is gone. The
research wrote those lines *about* the recipient in the third person — "their
listeners are mid-habit on daily Scripture" — which reads badly in a letter
addressed to that person, and the whole slot was a small sales pitch in a
message whose point is that there is no pitch. The opening line does the
personalising on its own.

## Notes on the wording

**"I'm not asking for a mention"** is meant literally. If it starts producing
coverage requests, the sentence stops being true and should be rewritten.

**The App Store link is the only link.** No tracking parameters, no campaign
tags, no link shortener. A shortener here would look like exactly the marketing
this is not, and it would let us count opens, which we have no business doing.

**No follow-ups.** There is no template B for "just checking in" and there
should not be one.
